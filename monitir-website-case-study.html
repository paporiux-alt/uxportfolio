Overview

Monitir · Digital risk protection · v1 website

Launching an AI security product
to buyers tired of AI hype

Monitir’s AI agents spot attacks on a brand and draft the takedowns. But security teams are drowning in alerts from tools that over-promise, and they don’t trust black-box AI. I designed the launch site to show the AI working, keep humans in control, and let buyers try it before a sales call.

Role
Lead Product Designer
Scope
Research → Story → Launch

Context

The product, and the catch

What Monitir does

AI agents find threats to a brand, link them into one attacker campaign, collect proof and draft the takedown. A person approves.

The catch

Security buyers have seen a lot of “AI” pitches. They’re skeptical of claims and wary of black-box automation.

Who reads the page

Analysts who want less noise, and CISOs who want ROI and an audit trail.

GoalTake skeptical buyers from “another AI tool” to “let me try it” in one scroll.

Research

Research

I started with four assumptions, then went looking for proof

No funnel data for a v1, and no buyers to interview. So I went back to 600+ G2 reviews of the tools they already use, and checked each assumption against what people actually complain about.

What 600+ G2 reviews complain aboutMentions
Too complex38
False positives36
Hard to learn25
Slow, noisy alerts24

Detection is solved. Signal, speed and trust are not. That line became the brief for the whole site.

Inspiration

Competitor research

What the market does well, and what it misses

I studied the tools buyers compare us with. Each one taught me something to borrow, and a gap to avoid.

CompetitorTheir + / −What I took for Monitir’s story
★ 4.4 on G2+ Broad coverage− Noisy alertsLead with signal, not volume: the Noise Funnel
★ 4.7 on G2+ Fast AI takedowns− Busy first screenShow agents acting, but a person approving
★ 4.5 on G2+ Links infrastructure− Hard to learnExplain linking visually: 4 signals → 1 attacker
Phishing specialist+ Fast phishing removal− Complex, costlyPut speed up front: “<6h median time to report”

Ratings and +/− are my read of public info and G2 reviews.

Visual inspiration

Patterns I borrowed from Landingfolio

I browsed SaaS and AI launches on landingfolio.com to see how the best sites make complex products feel simple. Five patterns made the cut.

  1. Real product in the hero

    Show the actual UI on the first screen, not an illustration.

    Used in · Hero
  2. Bento feature grid

    Mixed-size cards, each one job, scannable in seconds.

    Used in · How it works
  3. Stats + logo strip

    Hard numbers early build trust before any claim.

    Used in · Hero, Results
  4. Us-vs-them table

    One highlighted column makes the choice obvious.

    Used in · Why Monitir
  5. One dark ‘drama’ section

    A single dark block raises the stakes mid-scroll.

    Used in · Deepfake era
